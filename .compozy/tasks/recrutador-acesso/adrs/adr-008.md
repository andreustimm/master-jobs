# ADR-008: Administrators can revoke access but never grant it

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-06

## Context

Decided by the agent under the goal of 2026-10-06. Administrators can already
unlink a recruiter from a candidate (`app/admin/actions.ts:197-215`). G25 says
only the candidate creates the link. G24 says an administrator impersonating
someone loses administration actions.

## Decision

- Administrators keep revoking any active grant and cancelling any pending
  invitation from the administration area. The revocation follows the same
  rules as the candidate's (immediate, forward-only, recruiter notified,
  suggestions stay with the candidate) and is recorded in the candidate's
  history as "revoked by an administrator".
- Administrators never grant access or send invitations on a candidate's
  behalf, in the web or the CLI.
- An administrator impersonating a candidate cannot grant, revoke or resend
  either: the access page is visible read-only. Consent is the candidate's own
  act, and an administrator revocation must carry the administrator's name.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Administrators also grant

- **Pros**: Support can fix a candidate's setup.
- **Cons**: Violates G25; consent would not come from the data subject.
- **Why rejected**: G25.

### Alternative 2: Remove the administrator revocation

- **Pros**: Only the candidate touches access.
- **Cons**: No answer to an abusive recruiter or a compromised account.
- **Why rejected**: Revocation only reduces exposure, so it is safe to keep.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Safety valve preserved without creating consent on anyone's behalf.

### Negative

- None significant.

### Risks

- Impersonation used to grant; closed by denying grant actions in borrowed
  sessions.

## Implementation Notes

The existing administrator unlink switches from deleting the row to ending the
grant with a recorded reason.
