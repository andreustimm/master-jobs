# ADR-006: Invitation links last 7 days and are single use; caps on invitations and suggestions

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-06

## Context

Decided by the agent under the goal of 2026-10-06; the owner left the values
open. The invitation sends email to an address the candidate types, so it is an
abuse surface (spam, harassment through the product's domain). Drive's sharing
invitations stay usable for days. #464 already set sign-up caps (3 per IP per
hour) and code limits.

## Decision

- An invitation link is valid for **7 days** from sending and can be used
  **once**. After use, expiry or cancellation it shows a neutral "this
  invitation is no longer valid" page.
- Resending an invitation to the same email issues a new link and invalidates
  the previous one; the 7 days restart.
- A candidate can send at most **10 invitations and resends per rolling 24
  hours**, counted together; grants to existing recruiter accounts count too.
  Reaching the cap refuses further grants with a message saying when they can
  try again.
- A candidate can hold at most **20 pending invitations** at once.
- A recruiter can make at most **20 suggestions per candidate per rolling 24
  hours**.
- Values are product defaults; the TechSpec may make them configurable.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: 24-hour link

- **Description**: Short validity like a recovery link.
- **Pros**: Smaller window for a leaked link.
- **Cons**: Recruiters often read email days later; the link is bound to a
  verified email anyway.
- **Why rejected**: Binding to the email already limits misuse.

### Alternative 2: No expiry

- **Description**: Invitation valid until used or cancelled.
- **Pros**: Never fails for slow recipients.
- **Cons**: Old links pile up; stale consent.
- **Why rejected**: Consent should be recent.

### Alternative 3: No caps

- **Pros**: Simpler.
- **Cons**: The product's sender domain becomes a spam relay.
- **Why rejected**: Sender reputation and abuse.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Bounded email volume per candidate; recent consent.

### Negative

- A recruiter who misses the 7 days needs a resend.

### Risks

- Caps too low for an active candidate; values sit far above the expected use
  (a handful of recruiters).

## Implementation Notes

Invitation tokens are stored hashed, burned before access is created, and never
logged in deployments (same treatment as recovery tokens, G17/G18).
