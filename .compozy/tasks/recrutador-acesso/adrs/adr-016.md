# ADR-016: Expiry, grouped notices and purge run in the hourly sweep; the end date is local to the stored time zone

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-06

## Context

Decided by the agent under the goal of 2026-10-06; it also answers the PRD's
open question on the time zone.

Access is already denied at request time when `expires_at` passes
(ADR-012), but the status change, the history entry and the "access ended"
email need a worker. Production runs `pg_cron` → `/api/cron/varredura` →
`runSweepSlice` (`src/contexts/operations/app/sweep.ts`), whose
`atMostEvery(deps, key, intervalMs, work)` reserves a lease key at most once
per interval. The weekly `manutencao-banco.yml` workflow runs
`runDatabaseCleanup` (`src/core/db/retention.ts`). `auth_user` has `locale`
but no time zone.

## Decision

- `SweepDeps` gains `recruiterAccess(nowMs)`, called through
  `atMostEvery(deps, "manutencao:recruiter-access", 3_600_000, …)` at the end
  of every slice. It:
  1. ends grants with `status = 'active' AND expires_at <= now` as `expired`
     (conditional UPDATE … RETURNING, ADR-012), writes `access_expired`
     history and sends one "access ended — period over" email per grant;
  2. marks pending invitations with `expires_at <= now` as `expired` and
     writes `invite_expired` history (no email);
  3. sends the grouped suggestion email for each (recruiter, candidate) with
     un-notified `_by` rows whose last notice is at least one hour old
     (ADR-015 / PRD "one per recruiter per hour").
  Each step is idempotent: a second run finds nothing.
- `runDatabaseCleanup` gains `purgeDeadInvites(nowMs)`: deletes
  `recruiter_invite` rows in `expired | cancelled | superseded` older than 30
  days (history keeps the email; `invite_id` becomes null), and
  `recruiter_directory_query` rows older than one day.
- **Time zone:** the grant form sends the browser's IANA zone
  (`Intl.DateTimeFormat().resolvedOptions().timeZone`) in a hidden field,
  shown next to the date. The server validates it with `Intl` and falls back
  to `UTC` (shown as such) when missing or invalid. `expires_at` stores the
  UTC instant of `23:59:59.999` of the chosen date in that zone; `expiry_tz`
  keeps the zone for display and edits. There is no stored account zone to
  reuse.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Separate cron route

- **Pros**: Independent schedule.
- **Cons**: A new secret-guarded entry and a new `pg_cron` job.
- **Why rejected**: The sweep already runs hourly with leases.

### Alternative 2: Send expiry emails on the recruiter's next request

- **Cons**: A recruiter who never returns is never told.
- **Why rejected**: US-010.AC-2.

### Alternative 3: Account time zone setting

- **Pros**: One zone for everything.
- **Cons**: New setting and UI, outside the PRD.
- **Why rejected**: The zone at the moment of choosing is what the PRD asks.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Expiry email delay is at most about an hour, while the access cut is
  immediate (US-010.EC-1).

### Negative

- The sweep composition gains one dependency.

### Risks

- A failing email provider; each failure records `email_send_failed` and the
  state change still commits first.

## Implementation Notes

The sweep test (`tests/sweep-slice.test.ts`) asserts the key and the interval
with a fake lease; the store is exercised against Postgres.
