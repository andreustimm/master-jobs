# ADR-005: Both parties are notified by email, and the candidate sees who has access and the full history

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-06

## Context

LGPD art. 18 VII gives the data subject the right to know with whom their data
was shared. Consent must be recorded: who granted what, to whom, when. The
recruiter needs to know when access starts and ends. #464 delivers the
transactional email channel (Resend behind `Mailer`) with localized builders.

Decided by the owner on 2026-10-06.

## Decision

- Emails to the recruiter: invitation (no account), access granted (existing
  account or invitation completed), access ended (revoked by the candidate,
  revoked by an administrator, or expired).
- Email to the candidate: a new job suggestion arrived.
- On the account page the candidate sees the list of recruiters with access
  (email and name, granted on, end date or "no end date", last access) and
  pending invitations (email, sent on, link expiry).
- The candidate also sees a history of grants, invitations, invitation
  completions and expiries, revocations (and by whom), access expiries and
  suggestions (received, accepted, declined), newest first.
- History entries are kept for as long as the candidate's account exists.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: In-app notifications only

- **Description**: No emails; badges inside the product.
- **Pros**: No email dependency.
- **Cons**: A recruiter without an account cannot be reached; revocation goes
  unnoticed.
- **Why rejected**: Owner decision.

### Alternative 2: Current list only, no history

- **Description**: Show who has access now.
- **Pros**: Smaller screen.
- **Cons**: The candidate cannot answer "who has ever seen my CV?".
- **Why rejected**: LGPD art. 18 VII.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Consent and withdrawal are traceable by the candidate.

### Negative

- More emails to build and localize.

### Risks

- Email delivery failure; it never blocks grant, revocation or suggestion, and
  is recorded for administrators (as in #464).

## Implementation Notes

"Last access" is the last time the recruiter opened that candidate's funnel or
CV. Emails never contain the CV, the funnel or private data — only names, the
action and a link.
