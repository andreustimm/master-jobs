# ADR-002: Access scope is read-only funnel, current CV and job suggestions

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-06

## Context

Today a linked recruiter sees `/recruiter` (the candidates they follow) and
`/recruiter/[candidateId]` (a read-only funnel: job title, company, status,
dates), and gets 404 outside the link. They never see notes, CV, salary floor
or contacts. G21–G23 keep funnel, contact and salary floor away from public
surfaces. The candidate's funnel is the only place that records a person's
decision (rule 2), so nobody else may write it. Huntr lets a coach see the
board and add jobs; the owner wants the same "suggest" power without giving up
control.

Decided by the owner on 2026-10-06.

## Decision

A granted recruiter may:

- Read the candidate's funnel as today (title, company, status, dates,
  closed/archived marker), read-only.
- Read the candidate's **current** CV version, exactly as the candidate
  uploaded it.
- Suggest jobs to the candidate (ADR-003).

A granted recruiter never sees: candidate notes, salary floor, contact fields of
the profile, private analyses (score explanations, fit analyses, dossiers,
interview preparation), earlier CV versions, mail suggestions, or anything else
not listed above. The recruiter never edits the funnel, the profile or the CV.
Every grant gives this same scope; there are no per-recruiter roles.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Read-only, as today

- **Description**: Keep only the funnel view.
- **Pros**: No new surface.
- **Cons**: The recruiter cannot do the work they are invited for (evaluate the
  CV, bring jobs).
- **Why rejected**: The owner wants CV and suggestions.

### Alternative 2: Per-recruiter permission levels (viewer, contributor)

- **Description**: Drive-style roles chosen at grant time.
- **Pros**: Finer control.
- **Cons**: More decisions per grant for a single real use case.
- **Why rejected**: One scope covers the need; roles can come later.

### Alternative 3: Recruiter edits the funnel

- **Description**: Recruiter moves statuses on the candidate's behalf.
- **Pros**: Less work for the candidate.
- **Cons**: Breaks the rule that the funnel records only the person's decision.
- **Why rejected**: Rule 2 and owner decision.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Recruiter gets enough to help; private reasoning stays private.

### Negative

- The CV is shared as uploaded, so any contact data the candidate wrote in it
  goes along; the grant screen says so.

### Risks

- A future screen reachable by recruiters may leak a private field; mitigated
  by an allowlist of recruiter-visible fields and per-role end-to-end tests
  (G16).

## Implementation Notes

Authorization keeps deriving the link from the session (`linkedCandidateIds`),
never from input (G39/G40). Job pages opened from the recruiter view must not
show the followed candidate's score, notes or analyses.
