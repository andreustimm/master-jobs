# ADR-010: Keep the three profile visibilities and add a profile directory for recruiters

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-06

## Context

Candidate profiles have three visibilities (`VISIBILITIES` in
`src/contexts/auth/domain/types.ts`), offered on the candidate page
(`app/candidate/page.tsx`): Private, Recruiters and Public. The hint for
Recruiters reads "Authenticated recruiters in this system find and read your
profile". `can()` (`src/contexts/auth/domain/policy.ts`) lets any authenticated
recruiter read a profile set to Recruiters, but no screen lets a recruiter
*find* such profiles, so the promise in the hint is only half kept. Public
profiles are readable by anyone through `/p/[slug]`, limited to the
`publicProfile()` allowlist (G21), with the CV text only under a second consent
and filtered (G23).

Since #464, anyone with a verified email can sign up as a recruiter. Therefore
"Recruiters" means "anyone who signs up as a recruiter". The invitation in this
PRD (ADR-001) covers the opposite need: a named, revocable person who sees the
funnel and the CV.

Decided by the owner on 2026-10-06.

## Decision

- Keep the three visibilities with these meanings:
  - **Private:** nobody finds or reads the profile; only recruiters the
    candidate invited (this PRD) see it, within the grant scope.
  - **Recruiters:** any authenticated recruiter finds the profile in the
    directory and reads it.
  - **Public:** anyone with the `/p/[slug]` link reads it, and authenticated
    recruiters also find it in the directory.
- Add a **profile directory for recruiters**. Authenticated recruiters search
  by text (title, headline, skills) with simple filters among profiles set to
  Recruiters or Public.
- Directory results and profile reading show only the `publicProfile()`
  allowlist fields (G21). The CV text appears only with the candidate's second
  consent and through the same filtering as the public profile (G23).
- Never shown through the directory: funnel, applications, contacts, salary
  floor, notes, private analyses. These remain exclusive to the invitation.
- Private profiles never appear. Anonymous visitors cannot reach the
  directory. Finding or reading a profile there creates no link and grants
  nothing beyond the profile and its public link.
- **Accepted risk:** with open recruiter sign-up, Recruiters visibility reaches
  anyone who signs up as a recruiter. The candidate chooses it knowingly, and
  the scope is the same allowlist as the public profile.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Remove the Recruiters visibility

- **Description**: Keep only Private and Public; move Recruiters profiles to
  Private through a non-additive migration.
- **Pros**: The only way to reach a non-public profile is a grant.
- **Cons**: Takes away a discovery channel candidates chose, and needs a data
  migration with human review (ADR 0028).
- **Why rejected**: The owner wants candidates to be discoverable by
  recruiters without being fully public.

### Alternative 2: Restrict Recruiters to granted recruiters

- **Description**: The option means "recruiters I invited".
- **Pros**: No exposure to unknown recruiters.
- **Cons**: It duplicates the grant: invited recruiters already read the
  candidate. The option would mean nothing on its own.
- **Why rejected**: A setting with no effect of its own confuses the candidate.

### Alternative 3: Keep it as is, without a directory

- **Description**: Recruiters may read such profiles but cannot find them.
- **Why rejected**: The visibility promises "find" and delivers only "read by
  id".

## Consequences

### Positive

- Two distinct, explained channels: discovery (directory, allowlist only) and
  collaboration (invitation, funnel and CV).
- The Recruiters visibility does what its hint says.

### Negative

- Profiles on Recruiters are visible to anyone who signs up as recruiter.

### Risks

- A private field leaking into directory results; mitigated by reusing
  `publicProfile()` and `publicCvText()` and by per-role end-to-end tests
  (G16).
- Bulk harvesting by a self-signed-up recruiter; mitigated by pagination and a
  per-recruiter search rate limit defined in the TechSpec.

## Implementation Notes

No data migration. `can()` keeps allowing recruiters to read Recruiters
profiles, but limited to the allowlisted view. Reading the funnel or the full
CV still requires a grant.
