# ADR-007: Re-granting needs new consent, and revoking a pending invitation kills it

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-06

## Context

Decided by the agent under the goal of 2026-10-06. Two lifecycle gaps remained:
whether a revoked recruiter can be granted again, and what happens to an
invitation the candidate cancels before the recruiter accepts it.

## Decision

- After a revocation or expiry, the candidate may grant the same recruiter
  again. It is a new grant: new consent record, new date, new optional end
  date, a new access-granted email. Nothing from the previous grant (such as
  the end date) carries over.
- At most one active grant or pending invitation exists per candidate and
  recruiter email at any time; granting again while one is active or pending
  is refused with a message pointing to the existing entry.
- Revoking a pending invitation cancels it at once: its link stops working,
  opening it shows the neutral invalid-invitation page, and completing sign-up
  through it creates the recruiter account without any access. The recruiter
  gets no "access ended" email, because access never started; the history
  records the cancellation.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Block re-granting after revocation

- **Pros**: Prevents loops.
- **Cons**: Punishes the candidate for revoking by mistake.
- **Why rejected**: The candidate owns the decision.

### Alternative 2: Reactivate the old grant

- **Description**: Restore the previous record.
- **Pros**: One record per pair.
- **Cons**: Blurs when consent was given.
- **Why rejected**: Each consent is its own record.

### Alternative 3: Let a cancelled invitation still create access on completion

- **Why rejected**: Contradicts the candidate's latest decision.

## Consequences

### Positive

- The history reads as a sequence of explicit decisions.

### Negative

- The history of a long relationship may have several entries for one
  recruiter.

### Risks

- A sign-up in flight when the invitation is cancelled; the access check at
  completion time (not at link opening) closes it.

## Implementation Notes

The invitation's validity is checked when the account is confirmed, not only
when the link is opened.
