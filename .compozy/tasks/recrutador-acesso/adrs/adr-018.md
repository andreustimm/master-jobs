# ADR-018: Invitations open under `/signup` and complete when a recruiter account proves the invited email

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-06

## Context

Decided by the agent under the goal of 2026-10-06.

#464 builds `/signup` (task_03 of `login-social`, in execution): one screen,
social or manual path, Recruiter or Candidate, `email_verified_at` set when
the provider verifies the email or the code is confirmed. Recovery links
carry the token in the query string and check it before showing the form
(`app/login/reset/page.tsx`). Social sign-up leaves the site for the provider,
so state that must survive the round trip goes in an httpOnly cookie
(`flow-cookie.ts`).

## Decision

- **Page:** `GET /signup/invite?token=…` (public, registered in
  `PUBLIC_ROUTES`/`proxy.ts` and the entry inventory). It hashes the token and
  looks up a `pending`, unexpired invitation whose candidate account is
  enabled. Anything else renders the same neutral "no longer valid" page.
- **States of a valid link:**
  - no session → candidate's name, the masked expected address, "Create
    recruiter account" (Server Action `startInvitedSignup` stores the raw
    token in the httpOnly cookie `jho_invite`, `SameSite=Lax`, max-age =
    remaining validity, then redirects to `/signup?role=recruiter`) and "I
    already have an account" (`/login?next=/signup/invite?token=…`);
  - session with the invited email and the recruiter role → "Accept and open"
    (Server Action `acceptInviteAction`), a single confirmation click;
  - session with another email → "This invitation is for <masked email>. Sign
    out to continue."; nothing changes;
  - session with the invited email without the recruiter role → "This
    invitation needs a recruiter account"; nothing changes.
- **Completion** — `completeInvitesForAccount(userId, cookieToken | null)`
  runs in one transaction under the candidate lock (ADR-017) and:
  1. requires the recruiter role, an enabled account and a verified email
     (`email_verified_at`), or, for `acceptInviteAction`, the valid token for
     that same email, which proves the mailbox and sets `email_verified_at`
     when it was null;
  2. completes every `pending`, unexpired invitation addressed to the
     account's normalized email whose candidate account is enabled: burns it
     (`status = 'accepted'`, `accepted_user_id`, `decided_at`) **before**
     inserting the grant (G18), copies `access_expires_at`/`expiry_tz` to the
     grant, writes `invite_accepted` and `grant_created` history, and sends
     the access-granted email. An invitation whose `access_expires_at` already
     passed becomes `expired` and grants nothing (US-006.EC-5);
  3. reports the cookie's invitation as `completed`, `mismatch` (valid but
     for another email; the invitation stays pending), `not_recruiter`,
     `period_over` or `invalid`, and clears the cookie.
- **Hooks:** completion runs at the end of a recruiter sign-up (social and
  manual, after the account is committed) and after every successful sign-in
  of an account with the recruiter role. The landing is
  `/recruiter/<candidateId>` for `completed`, else
  `/recruiter?invite=mismatch|invalid|period_over`.
- **Dependency:** the hooks live in `src/contexts/auth/app/signup.ts` and the
  sign-up screen from `login-social` task_03. The task that implements this
  ADR starts only after that task is merged into `dev`.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Dedicated `/invite/[token]` sign-up

- **Cons**: A second sign-up screen to keep in sync with #464.
- **Why rejected**: PRD F2 says the link opens the #464 screen.

### Alternative 2: Accept on GET while signed in

- **Pros**: Zero clicks.
- **Cons**: A state change on GET, reachable by link prefetchers that carry
  cookies; CSRF-shaped.
- **Why rejected**: One confirmation click is the safer reading of
  US-003.AC-5 ("access starts at once" without a new sign-in).

### Alternative 3: Bind access to whoever uses the link

- **Why rejected**: ADR-001 binds consent to the typed, verified address.

## Consequences

### Positive

- A recruiter who signs up without the link still receives every valid
  invitation to that address (US-002.EC-7).
- Administrator-created recruiter accounts without `email_verified_at` can
  accept by clicking the emailed link.

### Negative

- The invitation task waits for `login-social` task_03.

### Risks

- Cookie left after an abandoned sign-up: it expires with the invitation and
  is ignored once the invitation is no longer pending.

## Implementation Notes

The raw token never reaches a log: the page and actions log only the
invitation id. The masked email keeps the first character of the local part
and the full domain (`a•••@x.com`).
