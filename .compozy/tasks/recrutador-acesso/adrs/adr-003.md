# ADR-003: Job suggestions are pending proposals that only the candidate turns into funnel entries

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-06

## Context

Rule 2 says ingestion writes only to `job`, never to `application`; anything
else produces at most a suggestion the person accepts, because the person's
decision is the only unrecoverable data. Recruiters can already register jobs in
the global catalog (`/jobs/new`, `job:write`). The owner wants recruiters to
suggest jobs without touching the funnel.

Decided by the owner on 2026-10-06.

## Decision

- A granted recruiter suggests a job to a specific candidate, either by
  choosing a job already in the catalog or by registering a new one with the
  existing recruiter job form, plus an optional short note.
- The suggestion stays **pending** until the candidate **accepts** (the job
  enters the candidate's funnel in the first funnel stage, `backlog`) or
  **declines** (nothing enters the funnel). Accepting or declining is final.
- The recruiter sees the state of their own suggestions (pending, accepted,
  declined) but never edits the funnel.
- Suggestions belong to the candidate: they stay with the candidate after the
  recruiter loses access, and the candidate can still accept or decline them.
- The candidate receives an email when a suggestion arrives.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Suggestion enters the funnel directly

- **Description**: The suggested job appears in the funnel with a "from
  recruiter" tag.
- **Pros**: One step less for the candidate.
- **Cons**: Someone else writes the funnel; violates rule 2.
- **Why rejected**: Rule 2.

### Alternative 2: Comments instead of suggestions

- **Description**: Recruiter comments on funnel entries.
- **Pros**: Richer collaboration.
- **Cons**: Not asked for; adds a moderation surface.
- **Why rejected**: Out of scope (Non-Goals).

## Consequences

### Positive

- The funnel stays a record of the candidate's own decisions.
- Revocation never destroys anything the candidate may still want.

### Negative

- The candidate must act on each suggestion.

### Risks

- Suggestion spam from a granted recruiter; mitigated by a per-recruiter daily
  cap and by revocation.

## Implementation Notes

Suggestions live apart from `application` and from `mail_suggestion`; accepting
creates the application through the same domain path the candidate uses today.
A job suggested twice to the same candidate is one pending suggestion.
