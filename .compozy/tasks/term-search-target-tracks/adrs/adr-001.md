# ADR-001: Saved Term Searches With an Immediate First Run

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-09-18

## Context

A candidate who searched the local corpus for `Laravel` or `PHP` found nothing
and wants the system to go get more jobs about a term — a technology such as
`php`, `Laravel` or `java`, or a role such as `techlead` — from the platforms
that are already registered. Research showed three facts that shape the answer:

- Only a few registered platforms search by term on their side. Remotive
  (`search`) is proven in the current integration. RemoteOK (`tag`,
  undocumented) and Himalayas (a separate search endpoint — the feed endpoint
  the integration uses ignores `q`) answered correctly in live tests on
  2026-09-18 but are not yet in their integrations. Applicant tracking boards
  (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby) return a whole company board that the daily sync
  already downloads.
- Platform terms cap repeated calls: Remotive asks for at most 4 calls a day and
  blocks above 2 a minute, and delivers postings with a 24-hour delay; Himalayas
  returns 20 results per request and answers 429 above that.
- No candidate-facing product (Teal, Careerflow, Simplify, Welcome to the
  Jungle) runs live federated searches against third-party boards. The market
  pattern is an indexed catalog plus saved searches that keep working. Applying
  within the first 96 hours materially raises interview odds, so a search that
  repeats is worth more than a snapshot.

## Decision

A term the candidate asks for becomes a **saved term search**. Saving it starts
the first run immediately against every registered, enabled platform that can
search by term; the candidate watches per-platform progress without the page
blocking. After that, the saved term repeats automatically with the daily
sweep and reports what is new. The candidate may also re-run a term by hand,
bounded by a cooldown. Jobs a term brings in are browsed and filtered on the
existing Jobs screen (ADR-005); there is no separate results page.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Live, one-off search only

- **Description**: Query the platforms when the candidate presses search, show
  the results, keep nothing.
- **Pros**: Simplest mental model; no stored state.
- **Cons**: A snapshot misses everything published afterwards; repeating it
  hits platform quotas fast; Remotive's 24-hour delay makes "live" no fresher
  than the daily sync.
- **Why rejected**: It trades the durable value (catching new postings early)
  for an immediacy the platforms cannot deliver.

### Alternative 2: Saved term that only runs in the next daily sweep

- **Description**: Store the term and let the scheduled sweep pick it up.
- **Pros**: Zero pressure on quotas; no progress surface.
- **Cons**: The candidate waits up to a day to see a first result, which feels
  broken right after asking.
- **Why rejected**: The first run is when the candidate is paying attention.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Matches the market pattern and the platforms' published terms.
- The candidate gets a first answer right away and keeps getting new jobs
  without coming back to ask.

### Negative

- Adds stored state (terms, run status) and a progress surface.
- The system needs a way to run captures outside the daily sweep.

### Risks

- A burst of new terms can exhaust a platform's daily quota; mitigated by the
  quota rules in ADR-004 and by showing "waiting for quota" instead of failing.

## Implementation Notes

How the first run executes in production (queue, scheduled runner, or workflow
dispatch) belongs to the TechSpec. The PRD only fixes the user-visible timing.
