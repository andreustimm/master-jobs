# ADR-006: Tracks and Terms Are Private; New Jobs Are Announced In-App Only

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-09-18

## Context

A track reveals a candidate's strategy and floor ("I accept USD 6k for PHP").
The project already treats the salary floor as a negotiating position that never
leaves on the public profile (`/p/[slug]`). The system has three roles (admin,
candidate, recruiter); administrators read private data only by impersonation,
which is recorded. The system can send e-mail (Resend, with terminal fallback),
but the candidate chose to be told about new jobs only inside the product.

## Decision

- Tracks, saved terms, run history and per-track compensation ranges are
  visible and editable **only by the owning candidate**.
- **Recruiters** see none of it, including for linked candidates.
- **Administrators** see aggregate operational health — per platform: calls
  used and remaining in the current window, failures, last error, count of
  active terms — without term text, compensation ranges or candidate identity.
  Any administrator run inspection shows term captures only in this aggregate
  form. Individual data is reachable only through recorded impersonation, whose
  session may edit tracks and terms but never starts a capture and keeps no
  bulk administrative action.
- Term text never appears in logs, command output or the daily sweep's review
  snapshot, which carry aggregates only (ADR 0018).
- Recruiter access to a linked candidate's resources never extends to tracks
  or terms; new permissions are owner-only and denied by default.
- The public profile never shows tracks, terms or floors.
- New jobs are announced **in-app only**: a per-term count of new jobs and a
  "new since your last visit" marker on the Jobs screen. No e-mail, no push.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Linked recruiter sees tracks without floors

- **Description**: A recruiter sees the candidate's tracks and term results to
  suggest jobs.
- **Pros**: Helps assisted job hunting.
- **Cons**: Exposes strategy; no current recruiter flow needs it.
- **Why rejected**: The candidate chose owner-only visibility.

### Alternative 2: Daily e-mail digest

- **Description**: One e-mail a day when saved terms find new jobs.
- **Pros**: Reaches the candidate within the 96-hour window without opening
  the dashboard.
- **Cons**: One more channel to manage.
- **Why rejected**: The candidate chose in-app only.

## Consequences

### Positive

- No new leak path for negotiating positions or strategy.
- No new outbound channel to secure or rate-limit.

### Negative

- A candidate who does not open the dashboard does not learn about new jobs.

### Risks

- Aggregate counts could identify a candidate on a single-user deployment;
  counts never include term text, so the exposure is limited to "N terms exist".

## Implementation Notes

New permissions follow the existing deny-by-default policy (`can()`), with the
candidate scope taken from the session, never from input.
