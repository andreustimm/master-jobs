# ADR-004: Candidate-Triggered Term Capture as a Bounded Exception to Admin-Only Ingestion

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-09-18

## Context

The `platform-catalog-hybrid-search` PRD (business rule 2) states that
`candidate` and `recruiter` cannot mutate the platform catalog or run
ingestion; captures are an administrator action. Saved term searches (ADR-001)
need a candidate's save to reach the platforms. At the same time:

- Today a source is identified by `kind:handle`; treating each term as a new
  source would make a term run close jobs of that "source" that did not come
  back, and the ingestion upsert would reassign a job's source to the term,
  after which the original source's sync no longer closes it.
- The neighbouring ADR-002 ("Separate Source Definitions From Search Scopes")
  already decided that a keyword or partial capture may add and refresh jobs
  but never closes unrelated ones.
- Platform terms bind the whole system, not each user (Remotive: at most 4
  calls a day; Himalayas: 20 results per request). The two keyword sources
  already configured on Remotive use 2 of its 4 daily calls and close jobs by
  absence — a partial window treated as a complete snapshot (backlog B-11).
- Ingestion is environment-gated (ADR 0021): production requires an allowlist,
  local requires opt-in, dev/staging/preview deny.

## Decision

A candidate may cause source captures **only through their own saved terms**,
and only in this bounded form:

1. **Which platforms**: registered, enabled platforms whose integration
   declares term search and passed validation against the real API. No
   platform is added, edited or enabled by a candidate. Platforms that cannot
   search by term are covered by the daily sync and by the corpus filter on the
   Jobs screen.
2. **Never destructive**: a term capture only adds jobs and refreshes jobs it
   returns. It never closes, archives or deletes a job, and never changes which
   platform a job is attributed to. A closed or archived job it returns reopens
   as ADR 0020 item 7 defines.
3. **System-wide quotas**: each platform's published limits apply to the whole
   system and count every call to the platform, regular sync included. The
   budget is durable and shared by every runtime. When it is spent, pending
   term runs wait for the next window and say so; they do not fail and do not
   exceed the limit.
4. **One call serves everyone**: the same normalized term on the same platform
   is fetched at most once per day; every candidate who saved it receives the
   result. Who saved which term stays private.
5. **Environment gate**: term captures run only where ingestion is permitted.
   Elsewhere the term is saved and its status says captures are off in this
   environment; no network call is made.
6. **Term normalization**: matching is case-insensitive and treats spaces and
   hyphens inside a term as equivalent (`Techlead`, `Tech Lead`, `tech-lead`
   are one term), with the scorer's word boundary. Synonyms (`Technical Lead`)
   are not expanded; they belong in a track's target titles.
7. **No runs under impersonation**: an impersonated session may edit tracks and
   terms but never starts a capture; its requests wait for the daily sweep.
8. **Same pipeline**: a first run or manual re-run uses the daily sweep's
   capture pipeline and the table-backed queue (ADR 0009); it is never a second
   pipeline.

This rule supersedes `platform-catalog-hybrid-search` business rules 1, 2 and 6
only for captures caused by a candidate's saved term; that PRD carries the
matching amendment. Everything else in those rules holds.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Keep captures admin-only; candidates request terms

- **Description**: A candidate submits a term; an administrator approves and
  runs it.
- **Pros**: Keeps the neighbour PRD's rule untouched.
- **Cons**: A human in the loop for every term; the first run waits for an
  administrator.
- **Why rejected**: The candidate's need is self-service and immediate.

### Alternative 2: Each term becomes its own source

- **Description**: Register `remotive:laravel` like any other source.
- **Pros**: Reuses the current sync path unchanged.
- **Cons**: Closure and source attribution break as described in Context.
- **Why rejected**: It violates the job-retention invariant (AGENTS.md rule 3).

## Consequences

### Positive

- Self-service without giving candidates any control over the platform catalog.
- Platform terms are respected by construction, including under many users.
- A term capture can never hide or lose a job.

### Negative

- Coverage depends on how many registered platforms search by term (three
  today); the screen must be honest about which platforms were searched.

### Risks

- A platform's undocumented parameter (RemoteOK `tag`) may change; each
  integration is validated against the real API before use (AGENTS.md "Ao
  adicionar uma fonte", step 4) and a failure is reported per platform.

## Implementation Notes

A candidate's private saved term resolves to a system capture profile per
platform and normalized term — the concept owned by the neighbour ADR-002 — so
one capture serves every candidate who saved the term. The quota ledger is
owned here and applies to every platform call. Today the ingestion upsert
always rewrites a job's source; the non-reassigning path of rule 2 depends on
the source identity work of backlog B-11.
