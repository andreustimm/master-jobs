# ADR-002: Open self-sign-up, choosing candidate or recruiter

> Amended the same day: self-sign-up also exists by email and password,
> confirmed by an emailed code (ADR-006), on the single screen of ADR-007.
> The rules below apply to both paths; "verified email" means verified by the
> provider (social) or by the code (manual).

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-05

## Context

Until now master-jobs had no public sign-up by design ("personal system, not a public sign-up"; `docs/product/vision.md` anti-vision: "not multi-tenant today"). Every account was created by an administrator. The owner decided that a social sign-in with an email that matches no account must create one, and that the new person must choose whether they are a candidate or a recruiter.

Security rules constrain what a new account may reach: G25 says a new account never points at an existing candidate and only the candidate creates a recruiter link; G24 keeps administration behind explicit roles.

## Decision

- A social sign-in whose (provider, subject) is unknown and whose verified email matches no account starts **self-sign-up**.
- Self-sign-up requires an email the provider asserts as verified. Without it, the person sees that sign-up needs a verified email and nothing is created.
- Before the account exists, the person chooses **Candidate** or **Recruiter**. Abandoning the choice creates nothing.
  - **Candidate**: the account is created with the candidate role and its own new, empty candidate profile (never an existing one), then lands on the existing "create my profile" onboarding.
  - **Recruiter**: the account is created with the recruiter role and no linked candidate; it lands on an empty state explaining that a candidate must grant access.
- The **admin** role is never self-assigned; it stays invite-only.
- Anyone with a verified email may sign up; the number of new accounts created per hour is capped product-wide, and administrators can disable any account.
- Granting a recruiter access to a candidate is out of scope (issue #465).

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Social login only for existing accounts

- **Description**: Unknown emails see "access not granted".
- **Pros**: Keeps the product closed, matching the prior vision.
- **Cons**: Every new user needs the administrator.
- **Why rejected**: The owner chose open sign-up.

### Alternative 2: Self-sign-up only as candidate

- **Description**: New accounts are always candidates.
- **Pros**: Simplest; no empty recruiter accounts.
- **Cons**: Recruiters still need the administrator.
- **Why rejected**: The owner requires the person to choose candidate or recruiter.

### Alternative 3: Allowlist of domains or emails

- **Description**: Only listed domains/emails may sign up.
- **Pros**: Tight control.
- **Cons**: Administrative upkeep; defeats open sign-up.
- **Why rejected**: New accounts expose no one else's data (a candidate sees only itself; a recruiter sees only who grants access), so the verified-email gate plus an hourly cap suffices.

## Consequences

### Positive

- New candidates and recruiters onboard without the administrator.

### Negative

- The product stops being invite-only; `docs/product/vision.md` and the first-access documentation must be updated to state that.
- Recruiter accounts are empty until issue #465 ships.

### Risks

- Account spam: mitigated by the verified-email requirement, the hourly cap, and administrator disable.
- Data exposure through a new account: mitigated by G25 (own empty candidate; recruiter with no links).

## Implementation Notes

The cap value is a product default recorded in the PRD (20 new accounts per rolling hour). Password-based and magic-link sign-in never create accounts.
