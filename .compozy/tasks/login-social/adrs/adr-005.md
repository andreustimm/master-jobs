# ADR-005: Social sign-in available in production and local only

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-05

## Context

master-jobs runs locally at `127.0.0.1:3000`, in production at `jobs.mastertimm.com.br`, and in Vercel Preview/staging deployments whose URLs change per deploy. Google and LinkedIn require pre-registered return URLs. The public origin used in links must come from configuration, never from the request host (G17), and Preview never receives a public URL.

## Decision

- The Google and LinkedIn buttons appear, and the social flow works, only where a fixed public origin and the provider credentials are configured: production and local.
- In Preview and staging the buttons are not shown and any attempt to start a social flow is refused; password and magic link keep working.
- A provider whose credentials are missing is hidden individually (for example, Google configured and LinkedIn not).

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Production only

- **Description**: No social sign-in locally.
- **Pros**: One registration per provider.
- **Cons**: The owner cannot exercise the flow locally.
- **Why rejected**: Local testing is part of the workflow.

### Alternative 2: Every environment with a fixed staging domain

- **Description**: Register a fixed staging domain and separate provider apps.
- **Pros**: Full parity.
- **Cons**: New domain and extra provider apps for little value.
- **Why rejected**: Not worth it now.

## Consequences

### Positive

- No broken buttons in Preview; no host-header-derived return URLs.

### Negative

- Social sign-in cannot be tested in Preview.

### Risks

- Misconfigured credentials hide a provider silently; the administrator status view should show which providers are active.

## Implementation Notes

The return URL is derived from the configured public origin.
