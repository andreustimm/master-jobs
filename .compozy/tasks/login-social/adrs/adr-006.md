# ADR-006: Manual sign-up confirmed by an emailed code, and a transactional email channel

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-05

## Context

The owner asked for the sign-up pattern of Tecla (`app.tecla.io/t/signup`): besides LinkedIn, a person can sign up with name, email and password; after submitting, they receive an email with a code that validates the sign-up, and after confirming they receive a welcome email. master-jobs has no email delivery today (issue #237, Resend, pending); email addresses are never verified, and password recovery cannot send its link.

An unverified email in a self-created account would undermine ADR-001: the automatic link trusts that the account's email belongs to whoever controls the inbox.

## Decision

- Manual sign-up (email and password) exists alongside Google and LinkedIn sign-up.
- A manual sign-up stays **pending** until the person enters a 6-digit code sent to the email. Only then is the account created and the person signed in. A pending sign-up cannot sign in and never takes part in automatic linking.
- Code rules: valid for 15 minutes; 5 wrong attempts invalidate it; a new code can be requested after 60 seconds, at most 5 codes per email per hour; requesting a new code invalidates the previous one. A pending sign-up not confirmed within 24 hours is discarded.
- Sign-up with an email that already has an account never reveals it: the screen shows the same "check your email" step, and the email received says an account already exists and offers sign-in and password recovery instead of a code.
- An account created by a confirmed manual sign-up has a verified email and may take part in automatic linking (ADR-001).
- The product gains a transactional email channel (bringing issue #237 into this scope) used for: sign-up code, welcome after the account is created (manual or social), notice when a provider is linked or unlinked (automatic, manual, by administrator or CLI), and the password-recovery link.
- Emails never contain provider tokens or profile data. The password-recovery link keeps G17/G18: origin from configuration, single use, one hour, never logged in deployments.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Social-only sign-up

- **Description**: New accounts only via Google or LinkedIn, whose emails arrive verified.
- **Pros**: No email delivery needed.
- **Cons**: People without Google/LinkedIn, or who prefer not to use them, cannot join.
- **Why rejected**: The owner wants the Tecla pattern with manual sign-up and code.

### Alternative 2: Manual sign-up without confirmation

- **Description**: Accept the typed email as is.
- **Pros**: No email delivery.
- **Cons**: Anyone could squat someone else's email; breaks automatic linking safety.
- **Why rejected**: Unsafe.

### Alternative 3: Confirmation link instead of code

- **Description**: Email with a clickable link.
- **Pros**: One click.
- **Cons**: Opens in another browser/device; the owner explicitly experienced and asked for a code.
- **Why rejected**: Owner's choice.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Every self-created account has a verified email.
- Password recovery and security notices finally reach people.

### Negative

- Depends on an email provider, a sender domain and its DNS records (owner's infrastructure).

### Risks

- Email abuse (sending codes to arbitrary addresses): mitigated by per-email and product-wide limits and the shared sign-up cap.
- Delivery failure: the screen offers "resend code" and states where to look; administrators can still create accounts as today.

## Implementation Notes

Provider credentials live in environment variables (rule 16/G41). In local and Preview, emails go to a development sink, never to real recipients, and codes are never written to logs in deployments.
