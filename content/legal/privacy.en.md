This Policy explains which personal data Master Jobs
(`jobs.mastertimm.com.br`) processes, why, and what your rights are under
Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018, "LGPD").

## 1. Who the controller is

The data controller is Andreus Timm, who runs Master Jobs. For any privacy
matter, including exercising your rights, write to
[contato@mastertimm.com.br](mailto:contato@mastertimm.com.br).

## 2. Which data we process

- **Account:** email, name, role (candidate, recruiter or administrator),
  language, your password stored only as a hash, the date your email was
  confirmed and the versions of these documents you accepted.
- **Sign-in with Google or LinkedIn:** we keep only the identifier the
  provider gives your account and the email it confirms as verified, with the
  date of the link and of the last use. To return the email, LinkedIn requires
  the basic profile permission, so its response also carries your name and
  photo: those two items arrive only during sign-in, are not stored, logged or
  used for anything, and are discarded when it ends. We do not receive the
  password, job title, contacts or posts of those accounts.
- **Candidate profile:** name, professional headline, CV and its versions,
  search preferences and, if you send them, the photo and cover of your public
  profile.
- **Application tracking:** the jobs you follow, the stage of each one and your
  notes.
- **Sign-up in progress:** the data filled in during sign-up while it is not
  completed (including the CV text) and the confirmation code, stored only as
  a hash.
- **Technical and security data:** records of sign-ins, sign-outs and failed
  sign-ins; the sign-up IP address, stored only as an irreversible code (HMAC)
  used to limit serial sign-ups; and error reports.

## 3. Why we use the data and on which legal basis

| Purpose | Legal basis (LGPD, art. 7) |
|---|---|
| Create and keep your account, authenticate you and send account emails | Performance of a contract (item V) |
| Estimate how well jobs fit your profile and organize your applications | Performance of a contract (item V) |
| Show your profile to a recruiter or at a public address, when you choose to | Consent (item I), which you can withdraw at any time |
| Protect accounts, limit abuse and investigate incidents | Legitimate interest (item IX) |
| Comply with legal obligations and respond to authorities | Legal obligation (item II) |

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

## 4. Who we share data with

We use providers that process data on our behalf, only to run the service:

- **Vercel** — application hosting and storage of profile images;
- **Supabase** — database;
- **Resend** — delivery of account emails;
- **Sentry** — server error reports;
- **Google and LinkedIn** — only to authenticate you, when you choose to sign
  in with them.

A recruiter sees your data only if you grant them access. We may also share
data when the law requires it.

## 5. International transfer

Some of these providers process data outside Brazil. In those cases the
transfer follows article 33 of the LGPD, with providers that adopt compatible
protection safeguards.

## 6. How long we keep data

- **Account:** while it exists. If you ask us to close it, we delete the
  account, its sessions and its links with Google or LinkedIn.
- **Candidate profile, CV and application tracking:** kept apart from the
  account and not deleted along with it. To have them deleted, ask for
  deletion explicitly (section 7), in the closing request itself or later; it
  covers everything except what the law requires us to keep.
- **Link with Google or LinkedIn:** until you disconnect the provider or close
  the account; disconnecting deletes the record.
- **Unfinished sign-up:** expires after 24 hours and is deleted by the weekly
  maintenance routine.
- **Completed sign-up record** (used for the per-IP limit): deleted after 30
  days.
- **Security records:** as long as needed to protect accounts and investigate
  abuse.

## 7. Your rights

Under the LGPD (art. 18), you can ask for: confirmation that we process your
data; access to it; correction of incomplete or outdated data; anonymization,
blocking or deletion of unnecessary data; portability; deletion of data
processed based on your consent; information about who we share it with; and
review of decisions made solely by automated means. You can also withdraw
consent and file a complaint with Brazil's National Data Protection Authority
(ANPD).

Make your request at [contato@mastertimm.com.br](mailto:contato@mastertimm.com.br).
Some of these rights you exercise directly in the service: editing your
profile, disconnecting a sign-in provider and turning off the public profile.

## 8. Security

Passwords and codes are stored only as hashes, traffic is encrypted (HTTPS) and
access to data is restricted to what is needed to run the service. No measure
is infallible; if an incident may put you at risk, we will notify you and the
ANPD, as the law requires.

## 9. Cookies

We use only cookies needed for the service to work: the session cookie, the
language cookie and a temporary cookie, valid for up to 10 minutes, that
protects sign-in with Google or LinkedIn. We do not use advertising or
tracking cookies.

## 10. Minors

The service is intended for people aged 18 or older.

## 11. Changes to this Policy

When this Policy changes, we will publish a new version on this page, with the
date at the top. Each account records the version accepted at sign-up.
